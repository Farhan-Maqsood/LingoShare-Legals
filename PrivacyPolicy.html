Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how DocNest currently handles information.

1. Introduction

DocNest is a document scanning and organization app for iPhone. This Privacy Policy explains what information the current version of DocNest does and does not handle. It applies to the DocNest iOS app.

DocNest respects your privacy. Your documents are meant to stay under your control.

2. Information We Collect

DocNest does not collect personal information such as your name, email address, or phone number. The current version does not create user accounts and does not send your documents to a DocNest server.

All scanned documents remain on your device unless you choose to export, share, print, or transfer them.

3. Information Stored Locally

DocNest stores documents and app settings locally on your device so you can scan, edit, search, and organize files. This information stays on your device unless you take an action that moves it elsewhere.

Local information may include:

  • Scanned pages and imported files
  • Cropped, filtered, or edited document images
  • Generated PDFs
  • Folders, names, and document metadata
  • OCR text extracted from pages you process
  • Signatures, stamps, watermarks, and annotations you add
  • Privacy Lock passcode settings, if you enable that feature
  • App preferences such as theme and tool settings
  • Downloaded on-device language resources used for OCR or translation

4. Features That Use Device Access

DocNest asks for device permissions only when a feature needs them:

  • Camera, to scan documents
  • Photo library, if you import images from Photos
  • Files, if you import or export documents
  • Face ID or Touch ID, if you enable Privacy Lock
  • Local network, if you use Nearby Transfer to send or receive files on your Wi-Fi

Nearby Transfer keeps files on your local network. Files are not uploaded to DocNest servers.

5. On-Device Processing

Features such as cropping, filters, OCR, translation, signatures, stamps, and PDF generation are designed to run on your device.

If you use OCR or translation, DocNest may download language resources to your device so those features can work. Those resources are used to process content you choose to scan or translate.

6. Sharing, Exporting, and Importing

If you share, export, print, or transfer a document, that file leaves DocNest according to the destination you choose, such as Mail, Files, AirDrop, a printer, or Nearby Transfer.

If you import a document from a link you provide, DocNest fetches that file from the source you entered. DocNest does not operate a cloud library of your documents.

7. In-App Purchases

DocNest uses Apple's secure in-app purchase system to process purchases or subscriptions. We do not store billing information, card numbers, or Apple account credentials.

Apple may process purchase information according to Apple's own privacy policy and App Store terms.

8. Analytics and Tracking

DocNest does not currently use analytics, advertising, or cross-app tracking.

9. Third-Party Services

The current version of DocNest does not integrate third-party analytics, ads, or tracking services that collect user data for advertising.

Purchases are handled by Apple. On-device text and language features may use Apple or vendor frameworks that download language resources to your device.

10. Children's Privacy

DocNest does not knowingly collect personal information from children or from any users.

11. Data Security

Because documents are currently stored locally on your device and are not transmitted to DocNest servers, we do not currently maintain a DocNest server containing your scanned files.

You can help protect your documents by keeping your device secure, using Privacy Lock if you want an extra passcode or biometric lock inside the app, and being careful when exporting or sharing files.

No method of storage is completely secure. If you delete the app, some locally stored documents and settings may be removed from the device.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if DocNest's functionality changes. When we update it, we will change the Last Updated date on this page. Please review this page from time to time.
