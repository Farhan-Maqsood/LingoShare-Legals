FAQ

Where are my documents stored?

DocNest stores scanned documents and folders locally on your device. Files are not uploaded to a DocNest cloud.

Does DocNest collect my personal data?

DocNest does not collect names, email addresses, or account information. See the Privacy Policy for details.

What happens if I delete the app?

Documents are stored locally. Removing the app may delete locally stored files unless you exported them first.

How do subscriptions work?

Purchases and subscriptions are handled by Apple. You can manage or cancel a subscription in your Apple account settings.

How does Nearby Transfer work?

Nearby Transfer sends files over your local network to a nearby phone or computer. Files stay on your Wi-Fi and are not uploaded to DocNest servers.

How can I report a bug?

Contact us at purenesttech@gmail.com and include your device model, iOS version, DocNest version, and a description of the issue.
