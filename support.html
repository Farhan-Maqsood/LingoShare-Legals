FAQ

Where are my pages stored?

Lingoshare keeps captured pages, translations, and folders on this device. They are not uploaded to a server operated by the developer. A file you export, share, or save in Files can also exist in that other place, including iCloud Drive if you put it there. The app can import from Files or iCloud Drive. The current Settings screen does not include a working library-sync switch.

Does the developer collect an account or my documents?

There is no Lingoshare account, and page images are not uploaded to the developer for storage. The app does include Google ML Kit. Google’s disclosure says those libraries can send diagnostics and usage analytics. Read the Privacy Policy before you treat the app as “no data leaves the device.”

What happens if I delete the app?

Pages stored only inside Lingoshare can be removed with the app. Export anything you need to keep first. Device backups may still hold an older copy, depending on your settings.

How do subscriptions work?

Lingoshare Plus is purchased from Apple. The product identifiers are com.fm.DocNest.Weekly, com.fm.DocNest.Monthly, and com.fm.DocNest.Yearly. Manage or cancel a subscription in your Apple account settings. The developer does not receive your card number.

How does Phone nearby work?

Phone nearby sends or receives files with another device on the same Wi-Fi. The transfer is local HTTP and is not encrypted. A 6-digit PIN starts the session. The QR code is an http link to the phone; the PIN is not written into that link. Files are not uploaded to a server operated by the developer. On a shared or public network, assume other devices can see the traffic.

How does Big screen work?

Big screen shows the page you select on an AirPlay display or a screen you connect with a cable. The page goes to that display. The developer does not receive or store the presentation. Anyone who can see the screen can see the page.

What are Key Facts and the bilingual handout?

Key Facts lists dates, amounts, phone numbers, addresses, and links found in the document on the device. Calendar and Reminders are used only if you choose to save a date. A bilingual handout is a PDF of the page beside, or above, its on-device translation. Check the result before you rely on it.

Why does a language need a download?

Translation packs download to the device, and the download may use cellular data. English does not need a pack in the current app. After a pack is on the device, translation of the text you choose is designed to run there, without an online translation API for that text. Google ML Kit may still send the diagnostics described in the Privacy Policy.

How can I report a bug?

Email purenesttech@gmail.com with your device model, iOS version, Lingoshare version, and a description of the issue.
